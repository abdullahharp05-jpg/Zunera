Hello, I'm Zunera Haroon

I'm a licensed psychologist with more than 16 years of experience across education, clinical counseling and institutional leadership. I currently support students dealing with academic stress, anxiety and depression, helping them build confidence, social skills and healthier ways to cope.

My clinical training includes an internship at a drug rehabilitation center in Rawalpindi, specialist training in CBT for substance use, and work with international programs on drug demand reduction. I'm currently pursuing my MPhil in Psychology, with research on culturally adapted CBT for women with substance use disorder.

My approach is simple: you set the pace, I listen without judgment, and together we work on what matters to you.
